# KURUKSHETRA Platform Vision

## Mission

KURUKSHETRA is a future-ready Enterprise Operational Intelligence Platform.

It is not a chatbot.

Its purpose is to unify organizational knowledge, operational workflows, monitoring systems, trackers, communication platforms, and specialized AI agents into one intelligent ecosystem.

---

## Core Components

KURUKSHETRA = Platform

SANJAYA = Planner and communication orchestrator

SEAL = Organizational learning framework

Agents = Specialized workers (Datadog, SQL, Smartsheet, Teams, Outlook, etc.)

Repository = Source of truth

---

## Architectural Layers

1. Ingestion
2. Knowledge Graph
3. Hybrid RAG
4. Context Assembly & Reranking
5. SANJAYA Planning
6. Tool Executors
7. SEAL Learning

Never merge responsibilities between layers.

---

## Future Expansion

Multiple teams may contribute independent agents.

Agents must communicate through SANJAYA rather than directly coupling with one another.

The graph is the shared organizational memory.

Documents remain immutable; learning creates new structured knowledge instead of editing originals.
